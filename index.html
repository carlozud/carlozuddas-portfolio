About me

My name is Carlo Zuddas, a biologist with a strong passion for photography.

My work focuses on movement, form and the expression of the body in space, with the aim of transforming every gesture into an essential image.

In sports photography, I look for something beyond the action: balance, rhythm and intensity, giving aesthetic value to every moment.

I work across portraits, landscapes and sport, with a careful eye for light, details and the power of the moment.

For me, photography is a way of observing and interpreting what happens.
